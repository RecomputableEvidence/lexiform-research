# LexiFORM

**Executable research in compositional, evidence-qualified relational semantics.**

LexiFORM is an independent NGAST research initiative examining how compositional interpretation, dependencies, relational claims, and evidence-qualified state transitions can be represented and tested in bounded executable environments.

## Research record

The prepared research baseline spans **LC-001 through LC-013**, with original archive identities, tests, historical qualifications, and negative results retained. The existence of an experiment, passing tests in stipulated synthetic semantics, and external semantic validity are separate claims.

The source baseline is being examined for public disclosure. **The preserved experiment archives have not yet been uploaded to this repository.** In particular:

- LC-009 is a **reconstructed runnable**, not the recovered original artifact.
- LC-012 (contested standing) and the separate LC-012 recomputation-audit branch are **different research objects**, despite overlapping sequence labels.
- LC-013 is a **separately identified successor**, not a retroactive amendment to LC-012's preregistered result.
- A new independent-oracle pressure experiment is a **proposal**, not an executed result.

## Research boundaries

The existing observations concern defined executable implementations and synthetic evidence rules. They do **not** establish universal semantic understanding, external truth authentication, LLM training, legal or organizational authority, or production readiness.

LexiFORM remains separate from Fork, Shared World, model-weight training, and other NGAST research programs. Shared vocabulary or evidence references do not create standing inheritance or cross-program validation.
