## Pass 1 — Lens Removal: LexiFORM as a whole

Without presuming that faster caching, longer audit chains, bigger alphabets, conventional symbolic equivalence, or historical packaging is the goal, the unresolved subject is: **a relationship between two assertions may itself be contested, conditional, scoped and historically revised.** Any implementation that makes newer claims automatically govern confuses time with justification. Any implementation that treats review admission as proof of physical truth makes a different error. The artifact must preserve original assertions, conflicting assertions, relationship claims, declared permissions, uncertainty, and the changing conclusions independently.

Resulting design boundary: **do not increase cryptographic packaging complexity to solve a problem of relational standing.** Retain LC011 unmodified; keep LC012 semantics self-contained. Refuse automatic newest-claim priority. Keep preserve-vs-amend separate.
