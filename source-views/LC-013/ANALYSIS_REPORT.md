## Executive finding

The archive is an evidentiary and executable **research lineage**, not a validated autonomously deciphered language, a trained LLM or a production authority system. Its LC experiments progress from a small compositional grammar through evidence-scoped execution, choice and lazy witness composition, incremental reuse, append audits, portable verification, relational continuity and finally contested relationships. Important distinctions are **observation vs truth**, **claim vs admitted relationship**, **rewrite vs historical reassessment**, and **content identity vs semantic integrity**. Newer claims do not inherently govern older observations. ARMORY-PPLX-EXP-001 and ARMORY-CIP-LIVE-001 are separate offline-tested integration packages; neither supplies independent provider execution in the provided archive.

**Reproducible subject-level defect:** LC012 treats pending `DISPUTES` and `ADDS_CONTEXT` links as possible defeat edges and forces ATOM scalar `U`, despite its own candidate contract describing them as non-defeating. Before approval, an unadmitted dispute/context annotation changes `T` to `U`; after admission it returns to `T`. Independently, an unrelated target's admitted annotation modifies the queried target's `qualifiers` and `support` provenance. These are semantic and evidence-scope differences, not just formatting. Agreement among LC012's three implementations does not independently validate an erroneous shared policy implementation.

**Constructed successor:** `LC-EXP-013-SCOPED-RELATIONSHIP-001` is a separate package preserving an exact LC012 predecessor ZIP. It distinguishes pending attacks from pending nondisplacing metadata, and scopes qualifier/support projection by the queried target's link IDs across the list, typed, and finite-set oracle paths. All **33** local test groups passed: 26 inherited LC012 tests (including 512 expressions, 128 scenarios and 32 order shuffles) and seven targeted regression tests. Three predecessor probe findings did not reproduce under those targeted successor fixtures. This is not broader correctness or external evaluator replication.
