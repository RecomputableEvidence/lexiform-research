## Executed behaviors (see full `results/EXAMINATION.md`)

1. Evidence ingress validates uniqueness, time types, interval bounds, explicit persistence policies, and atom shape. Rejects colliding IDs and invalid/mislabeled definition versions.
2. Observation-stage, claimed validity interval, query-stage, and knowledge-stage are separately expressed and included in result traces. Expired claims do not automatically transfer into present scope.
3. Independent AST-DAG and stack VM evaluate the same typed expressions and produce matching semantic projections over tested inputs. Their **indexed evidence lookup is shared**, a declared limit to independence.
4. `REWRITE` and `REASSESS` create separate hash-bound receipts. Prior expressions and evaluation outputs are not rewritten. Reassessment permits evidence append, not silent deletion or amendment of IDs.
5. Structural interning, in-process result cache, and indexed evidence lookup have instrumented cold/warm comparisons with an equally indexed typed-AST baseline. Warm cache avoids repeated evidence selection; it **does not outperform the matched baseline** on this benchmark.
